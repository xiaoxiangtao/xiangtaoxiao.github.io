Understanding the Transformer, From a Product Manager's Angle

Transformer: From First Principles to a Product Lens 📚 Why does the Transformer matter so much? The Transformer architecture came out of Vaswani et al.’s 2017 paper “Attention Is All You Need.” It changed NLP completely and is now the backbone behind every major LLM — GPT, BERT, LLaMA, you name it. The core idea: drop CNNs and RNNs entirely and rely only on the attention mechanism to capture dependencies across a sequence. That single move solved two problems at once: Parallelism: RNNs process tokens one at a time; attention lets you process the whole sequence at once, which speeds up training enormously Long-range dependencies: RNNs/LSTMs tend to lose the signal over long sequences (vanishing gradients); attention doesn’t have that problem ...
