I am looking for a six-month internship working on AI products or applied AI, and I am available immediately.

I understand commerce, payments and procurement, including how buyers, suppliers and platforms interact and how order, account, payment and fulfilment systems fit together. Product experience#

Risk analysis and investigation tools (Apr – Jul 2026)

  • Led work to identify accounts used by manufacturers to monitor suppliers’ prices, while avoiding restrictions on legitimate pharmacy buyers who shared accounts or devices.
  • Compared five rule configurations on 3.37 million records, combining more accurate IP location data with trusted-device filtering to increase risk-user detection by 6%.

E-commerce platform (Jan 2024 – Apr 2025)

  • Translated chain-pharmacy purchasing needs into a smart procurement workflow that matched a 300-item list in about 30 seconds and added roughly 600 key-account customers within a year. Also coordinated ERP integration with chain headquarters.

Payments and settlement (Apr 2021 – Jan 2024)

  • As the sole product manager for payments, led the migration to a bank-managed settlement solution with Ping An Bank, defining account structure, fund allocation and reconciliation workflows.

  • Saved RMB 30.7 million in net payment processing costs in 2023 by negotiating provider rates, reworking payment routing and encouraging lower-cost payment methods.

  • Used event tracking, user journeys, surveys and interviews to find friction in new payment methods, and worked with Alipay to improve the payment flow.
  • Designed payment-channel monitoring and automated or one-click responses, bringing anomaly detection, alerts and handling to under a minute.
